Ripple Pond Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Ripple Pond does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings are saved in your browser's local storage so the pond remembers them next time: sound style, volume levels, rain on or off, time of day, and your breathing and sleep timer lengths. They never leave your device. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Let go

The words you write in Let go are drawn on the paper boat and then discarded when the boat sinks. They are never saved and never sent anywhere.

Working offline

After your first visit, your browser keeps a copy of the game's files so the pond can open without an internet connection. These are only the game's own files, not information about you.

Device features

  • Vibration: a very light tap when you drop a pebble, on phones that support it.
  • Screen wake lock: keeps the screen on during sleep mode only, and releases it when the timer ends.
  • Ripple Pond never uses your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Hosting and app stores

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser downloads the files, to keep the service secure and running. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you install Ripple Pond from an app store, that store may collect information under its own privacy policy. The game itself still collects nothing.

Children

Ripple Pond is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. For questions, open an issue on github.com/ThinukaLehan/ripple-pond.

Ripple Pond is a calm koi pond to relax in. No score, no timer, no way to lose.
